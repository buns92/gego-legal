# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

GEGO (Geckos on the Go) respects your privacy.

To provide gameplay features, GEGO may store:

- Reddit username
- Leaderboard scores
- Daily puzzle progress
- Gameplay statistics

This information is used solely to operate the game and improve the player experience.

GEGO does not sell or share personal information with third parties.

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through Reddit.
